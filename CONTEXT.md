# Nexus Protocol

Nexus Protocol is a browser isometric squad-tactics game where the player is the corporation and agents are inventory. The diegetic vocabulary is the Lexicon in `docs/game-design.md` Section 21; this glossary pins the terms whose cross-document usage the Lexicon leaves ambiguous.

## Language

**Browser AAA**:
The ship bar (R5): an experience-quality claim that the mission frame, feel, audio, and interface read as AAA game quality from a shareable link, claimable only once the measured R5 definition of done passes. Unqualified "AAA" in project docs means exactly this claim.
_Avoid_: industry AAA (never a goal or claim), AAA as a statement of package scope, team size, or content volume

**Premium threshold**:
R3-rung quality per visual scorecard category; the floor no category may fall below during R4/R5 work.

**Asset**:
An agent, framed as inventory; unqualified "asset" means this. Objective objects are "marked assets" or "mission assets"; production files are "art assets".

**Doctrine**:
Deliberately dual-scoped: a rival syndicate's signature tactical identity, and one of the four named player approaches (loud assault, persuasion swarm, ghost, vehicular). Qualify as "rival doctrine" or "player doctrine" wherever the scope is ambiguous.

**Unrest**:
Territory-level instability driven by tax rate, exclusively a meta-layer stat; crossing its threshold flips the territory neutral. Any in-mission pressure meter (the Counter-Broadcast meter, for example) takes its own name.
_Avoid_: district unrest, mission unrest

01

Campaign vitals bar

.maphud

The header strip. A single scan-line of the whole campaign economy, so the player can decide "can I afford this / when do I get paid" without leaving the map.

rendered · top of screen, above the region tabs

GLOBAL OPERATIONS

1400cr +2424cr/hr R&D LABS 1/1 ACT 1 CYCLE 0 · next in 27m
Used in
Top of the worldMap screen, above the region tabs. Built by mapHud. screens.ts:253
Communicates
Treasury, passive income rate, committed lab capacity, story act, and the countdown to the next economy payout, all at a glance.
Fields
  • 1400cr — treasury, m.credits
  • +2424cr/hr — incomePerCycle(m) * 2 (a cycle is 30 min, so per-cycle is doubled to per-hour)
  • R&D LABS 1/1 — m.active.length of m.labSlots lab slots committed to running projects
  • ACT 1 — campaignAct(m), story progression 1–3
  • CYCLE 0 · next in 27m — m.cycle plus ceil((CYCLE_MS - m.econMs)/60000); pushed to the right edge via .cyc
Data
MetaState. The economy runs in real time: econMs accrues while you sit on this screen, so the countdown ticks and income pays out live.
States
Static display. Credits render amber; everything else is muted. Wraps onto multiple lines on narrow viewports. No interaction.
02

Region tabs

.regiontabs

Switcher across the eight regions of the world (5 territories each). Doubles as a progress read: the n/5 count shows how much of each region you hold.

rendered · below the vitals bar
Used in
Region navigation row of worldMap. screens.ts:340
Communicates
Which region's sectors fill the district stage below, plus ownership progress per region and which regions are still locked.
States
  • active — the selected region, class primary (cyan fill)
  • unlocked — clickable, shows NAME n/5 owned count
  • locked — disabled, shows NAME [LOCKED]
Interaction
Click sets this.selRegion, clears the current selection (this.selTerr = -1), and re-renders the whole screen. Handled by delegation on data-region. The stage's unlocked-neighbour gateway (component 06) fires the same handler.
Data
REGIONS list; owned count from m.territories; lock state from regionUnlocked(m, i) vs m.regionsUnlocked.

Interaction

Used in
Foot of the worldMap screen. screens.ts:357
Communicates
The most recent cycle payout, territory transfer, siege event, or charter milestone — so a returning player sees the latest change without a wall of log lines.
Data
m.log[0] — newest entry only, prefixed with >. The last four entries are joined into the element's title, surfaced on hover; full history stays in the debrief screen.

How it's all wired

There are no per-component event listeners. The whole screen is one innerHTML template assembled from string-builder helpers (mapHud, stage, terrBlock, popover, rdDrawer) with exactly two delegated handlers on the #screen element. onclick dispatches, in priority order, on data-contract / data-defend (mission handoff), data-region (switch region, via closest so the stage gateway works too), then data-sel (select a block, matched with closest('[data-sel]') because blocks have children); a click on empty stage clears the selection. oninput dispatches on data-tax. Selection state lives on the Screens instance (selRegion, selTerr) so they survive the whole-screen re-render that runs on every state change and on the 60-second economy tick. This innerHTML-plus-dataset pattern is the shared convention for every screen in src/app/screens.ts — follow it when adding components.

Specimens reproduce the in-game dark skin, since they represent a fixed in-game surface. Data and line references from src/app/screens.ts and src/app/meta.ts.

Part 2

Field Reference — Controls

The operative keymap for a live mission: mouse, squad, stims, gear, crowd control, and camera. For the colour of every actor these controls act on, see Unit Roster.

Mouse

LMB Select agent under cursor Click empty ground to fall back to A1 LMB drag Box-select squad Shift+LMB Add to current selection RMB Move to ground / attack hostile under cursor RMB On a vehicle: walk over and board it Ctrl+RMB Attack the vehicle instead Cmd works too. Fuel pumps chain-react Wheel Zoom

Squad

1234 Select agent Shift adds to selection, double-tap centers the camera on them 5 Select every living agent Tab Cycle equipped weapon R Cycle rules of engagement HOLD never auto-fires, DEF engages at half weapon range, FREE at full range F Persuade pulse Needs an agent carrying the Persuadertron J Hijack a nearby vehicle

Stims

Z Combat Take 15% less damage per level, weapon spread widens 30% X Focus Spread tightens 25% per level, move speed drops 15% C Surge Move 30% faster per level, burns HP while active

Each tap cycles that stim through levels 0, 1, 2. Any active stim drains the shared reserve (the R number on the HUD); when it empties, all stims cut out at once.

Gear

V Cloak T Plant demo charge Y Deploy medbay U Launch drone K EMP burst

Gear keys only work for selected agents actually carrying that equipment. Charges breach storefronts, opening new routes.

Crowd Control

G Swarm: follow the squad H Swarm: hold position B Flashmob at the cursor

Orders apply to every persuaded civilian. Influence (HUD, top right) counts your current following.

Camera and Sim

WASD Pan Arrow keys work too, relative to camera facing QE Rotate view in 45 degree steps [ and ] are aliases Space Pause -= Sim speed down / up 50% to 100%

Defense contracts: P toggles placement, click drops a turret, shift-click a trap, Enter finishes.

Basic Movement

  • Right-click moves the squad. Agents pathfind around buildings on their own; orders outside the map clamp to its edge.
  • Vehicles beat walking. Right-click one to board, J to hijack, then right-click a road destination. No road, no route.
  • Alert escalates GREEN to AMBER (police) to RED (tacticals). Gunfire and witnesses feed it.
  • Breaches are shortcuts. Demo charges and explosions open storefront walls, and paths through them stay valid.
  • Watch the reserve. Stims trade the shared R meter for an edge; Surge also eats HP, so switch it off after the sprint.
  • Lost an agent offscreen? Double-tap their number key to snap the camera to them.
